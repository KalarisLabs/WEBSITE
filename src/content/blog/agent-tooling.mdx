When non-computer-scientists attempt to reproduce recent computational biology or machine learning papers, more than seventy percent of total hours are consumed by dependency collisions, CUDA drivers, and container incompatibilities.

Our agent runtime abstracts this friction into declarative environments that synthesize ephemeral sandboxes directly from repository dependencies.
