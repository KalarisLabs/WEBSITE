Traditional research tooling resets context at the beginning of every experimental cycle. Hypotheses, environment configurations, and failed runs vanish into local terminal logs.

Recursive self-improving infrastructure treats each experiment not as a standalone task, but as a training signal. By accumulating structured context across repositories, papers, and execution traces, subsequent research cycles begin with higher initial velocity.
