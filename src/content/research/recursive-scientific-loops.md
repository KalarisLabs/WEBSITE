## Abstract Summary

Current agentic workflows suffer from uncalibrated drift and non-reproducible intermediate reasoning traces. In this paper, we formalize the recursive research evaluation framework: a discipline of observation, hypothesis evaluation, and deterministic checkpointing that ensures agent-driven scientific workflows remain verifiable.
